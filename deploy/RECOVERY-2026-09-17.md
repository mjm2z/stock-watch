# StockWatch recovery — September 17, 2026

## Verified production failures

- Strategy `sp500-long-v0` remains `development`; the paper-order gate correctly
  prevents all orders. Timers alone did not activate paper trading.
- 25 scan jobs failed. Recent attempts stop at `immutable bar changed for A at
  2026-08-31T04:00:00Z`. Collection fails before any signals are calculated.
- Those scan rows remained `queued`, hiding the actual failure on the homepage.
- `/api/stock/AAPL/quote` returns `FINNHUB_API_KEY environment variable is not set`.
  Web quotes/search/watchlist use Finnhub, while the worker uses configured Alpaca.
- The September 17 SEC refresh stopped at FITB (190/503): SQLite was locked.

## Repair

- Alpaca/IEX read-only quotes, search over the local instrument catalogue, and
  charts; homepage market cards refresh every 30 seconds. Charts refresh every
  minute, preserve intraday times and keep the current zoom during refresh.
  Missing data remains unavailable. Prices show the provider observation time.
- Corrections explicitly enabled for scheduled scan collection preserve before
  and after values in `market_bar_revisions`. Strict ingestion remains the
  default for existing historical workflows. Successful scans freeze their
  returned bar series in `scan_bar_snapshots`; retrying them reuses those inputs.
  Completed features, signals and backtest artifacts are not rewritten.
- Collection failures propagate to scan status. Additive migration 010 corrects
  existing failed-job/queued-scan mismatches without retrying old work.
- SQLite writers wait up to 30 seconds for contention rather than five seconds.
  Existing fundamental refresh resumes by skipping recent completed documents.
- Historical failures remain visible but do not permanently mark a subsequently
  recovered scanner as unhealthy. Inactive paper trading has an explicit banner.

## Installation

`sudo bash /home/mjm2z/stock-watch-repair-20260917/deploy/repair-paper-root.sh`

The launcher starts `stock-watch-repair.service` independently of SSH and returns.
Inspect `journalctl -u stock-watch-repair --no-pager -n 40`. It refuses to interrupt
an active database backup; preserves enabled timers, rollback source/environment,
and backup-directory ACLs; builds and migrates; checks deployed quote/chart APIs;
refreshes SEC facts; and runs a fresh development scan with no order submission.

Only a successful scan of at least 450 candidates and matched paper-account
reconciliation permits cloning the unchanged strategy to `sp500-long-paper-v1`
and selecting it in the environment. Failed validation leaves the old selected
strategy in place and logs the reason. Normal timers are restored on failure.

Paper orders start at the next scheduled trading-day scan (9:45 AM / 4:15 PM
America/New_York), only for qualifying signals. Existing $5–$15 order sizing,
$30 ticker limit, deduplication and paper-endpoint guards remain in force.
The installer does not replay historical scans or send real-money orders.

Rollback source and original environment are retained under
`/var/backups/home-ops-code/stock-watch-repair-TIMESTAMP/`. Migration 010 is additive;
rollback needs a rebuild of the saved source, not a destructive database restore.

The update is staged until the administrator runs the command. A production
scan and provider checks are required before describing trading as operational.
